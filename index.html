The Designer

Chichi sees beauty
as a discipline,
not an accident.

Born and raised in Nairobi, Chichi has spent over eight years cultivating an eye for interiors that are at once sophisticated and deeply liveable. She doesn't just furnish rooms — she composes atmospheres.

Her work sits at the intersection of design and craft. She collaborates closely with master furniture makers, translating her visions into singular, handcrafted pieces that belong nowhere else but the space they were made for.

"I don't believe in furniture that simply fills a room. I believe in pieces that complete it."

— Chichi, Founder
Our Philosophy

Every room holds a
story waiting
to be designed.

01

The Eye First

Design begins before a single measurement is taken. We study your light, your life, your movements through a space — then we begin.

02

Craft Over Catalogue

We partner with master furniture makers who understand that a piece built for a specific room will always outlast one built for any room.

03

Quiet Luxury

Exquisite design doesn't announce itself. It simply makes you feel, the moment you enter, that everything is exactly as it should be.

How We Work

From vision
to the room
you live in.

01

Consultation

We meet, we listen, we look. A thorough conversation about your space, your life, and the feeling you want to come home to.

02

Design Concept

Chichi develops a full spatial concept — mood, materials, proportions — before a single piece is specified or ordered.

03

Artisan Commission

We brief our furniture partners — skilled craftspeople who build each piece by hand, to the exact specifications the design demands.

04

Installation

Every element arrives, and Chichi personally oversees the final installation — because the last detail matters as much as the first.
